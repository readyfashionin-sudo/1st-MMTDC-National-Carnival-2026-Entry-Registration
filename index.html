Questions

Where is the carnival?

At Motijheel Model School & College, on 14 and 15 December 2026.

How do I pay the entry fee?

Send 100 tk by bKash (Send Money) to the number shown in the fee box above, and keep the TrxID from the confirmation message.

How will I know I am registered?

After you submit the form, send the generated details to the organizers on WhatsApp. Your registration counts once they receive them.

I made a mistake in the form

Can I register more than one person?

Yes. After submitting, tap Register another person. Each person registers separately.
